# Adapt Subject Context

Load only the reference matching the selected subject.

## Routing

- Management and business → `references/management.md`
- Economics and finance → `references/economics-finance.md`
- Computing and information systems → `references/computer-information.md`
- Law and public policy → `references/law-public-policy.md`
- Engineering and technology → `references/engineering.md`
- Education → `references/education.md`
- Medicine and health → `references/health.md`

For an unknown subject, use the closest reference only as a structural guide and explicitly preserve the teacher's terminology.

## Workflow

1. Identify the course concepts actually needed to reason about the case.
2. Select credible roles and evidence types for the discipline.
3. Match terminology and evidence complexity to the learner level.
4. Separate observable facts, stakeholder claims, assumptions, and missing information.
5. Mark unsupported organizations, people, events, and figures as teaching fiction.
6. Do not invent citations, confidential data, or high-stakes professional conclusions.
