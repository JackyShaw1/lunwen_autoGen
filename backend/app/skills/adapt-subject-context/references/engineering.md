# Engineering and Technology

- Roles: design engineer, operator, safety lead, supplier, client, regulator, or community representative.
- Evidence: specifications, failure modes, tolerances, test results, maintenance history, cost and schedule constraints.
- Decision variables: safety, performance, reliability, manufacturability, lifecycle cost, and environmental effect.
- Common failure: propose a solution with no measurable requirements, verification plan, or trade-off.
- Safety: do not treat fictional calculations as sufficient for real-world engineering approval.
