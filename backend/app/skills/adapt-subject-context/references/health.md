# Medicine and Health

- Roles: patient, family, clinician, nurse, pharmacist, administrator, public-health team, or ethics committee.
- Evidence: de-identified symptoms, timeline, tests supplied by the teacher, capacity limits, protocol and communication record.
- Decision variables: benefit, harm, uncertainty, consent, equity, resource use, and continuity of care.
- Common failure: present a fictional diagnosis or treatment as authoritative, omit uncertainty, or expose identifiable patient details.
- Safety: keep cases educational, de-identified, and non-prescriptive; require expert review before classroom use.
