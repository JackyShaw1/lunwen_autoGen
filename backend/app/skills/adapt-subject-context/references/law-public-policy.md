# Law and Public Policy

- Roles: parties, counsel, agency, court, regulator, public group, or affected citizen.
- Evidence: facts, chronology, rule text supplied by the teacher, procedural record, competing interpretations.
- Decision variables: authority, rights, duties, procedure, proportionality, enforceability, and public impact.
- Common failure: invent statutes or cases, merge disputed allegations with established facts, or state one interpretation as settled law.
- Safety: mark the scenario educational and fictional; require jurisdiction and date for real-law claims.

## Contract-law minimum structure

- State jurisdiction, contract date, parties and legal relationship.
- Quote or accurately summarize the clauses that create the dispute; include performance, acceptance, change, termination and liability terms when relevant.
- Present a chronology and distinguish agreed facts, allegations, disputed facts and facts requiring proof.
- Build both sides' claim-rule-fact-evidence-defense chains before reaching a conclusion.
- Require authoritative rule sources and their effective dates for real-law propositions. Do not invent article numbers or precedents.
- Compare legally available remedies and procedural choices rather than reducing the task to a generic fairness discussion.
