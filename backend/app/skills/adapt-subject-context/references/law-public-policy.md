# Law and Public Policy

- Roles: parties, counsel, agency, court, regulator, public group, or affected citizen.
- Evidence: facts, chronology, rule text supplied by the teacher, procedural record, competing interpretations.
- Decision variables: authority, rights, duties, procedure, proportionality, enforceability, and public impact.
- Common failure: invent statutes or cases, merge disputed allegations with established facts, or state one interpretation as settled law.
- Safety: mark the scenario educational and fictional; require jurisdiction and date for real-law claims.
