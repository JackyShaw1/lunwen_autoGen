# Economics and Finance

- Roles: household, firm, regulator, investor, lender, market intermediary, or policymaker.
- Evidence: prices, quantities, elasticities, cash flows, scenarios, incentives, distribution effects.
- Decision variables: efficiency, risk, liquidity, opportunity cost, externalities, and equity.
- Common failure: claim causality from one correlation or use unexplained fabricated statistics.
- Safety: label synthetic data; do not present investment, tax, or regulatory conclusions as professional advice.
