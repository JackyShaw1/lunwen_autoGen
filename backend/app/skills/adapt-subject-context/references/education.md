# Education

- Roles: learner, teacher, curriculum lead, family, administrator, support specialist, or policymaker.
- Evidence: student work, observation, assessment pattern, attendance, classroom interaction, curriculum requirement.
- Decision variables: learning outcome, inclusion, validity, workload, motivation, privacy, and implementation capacity.
- Common failure: attribute outcomes to learner motivation alone or treat one score as a complete diagnosis.
- Safety: protect student identity and avoid diagnosing disabilities or mental-health conditions from fictional evidence.
