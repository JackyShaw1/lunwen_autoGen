# Management and Business

First identify the course family. Do not treat operations research, accounting, finance, strategy, marketing, and organizational behavior as interchangeable management stories.

- Roles: accountable executive, functional manager, frontline operator, customer, partner, or board.
- Evidence: KPI trends, process delays, employee feedback, budget, customer outcomes, meeting records.
- Decision variables: strategy fit, incentives, capability, governance, adoption, resource allocation.
- Common failure: generic “resistance to change” with no incentive, workload, authority, or information mechanism.
- Safety: fictionalize organizations and figures unless sources are supplied.

## Operations research and management science

- Required entities depend on the model: decisions, parameters, objective, constraints, feasible alternatives, data table, and interpretable results.
- Stochastic programming additionally requires decision stages, random variables, scenarios with probabilities, recourse variables, and a comparison with a deterministic or risk-adjusted solution.
- Evidence should be numerical and internally consistent. A conflict between managers is not a substitute for a model or dataset.
- The student task must include formulation, calculation/comparison, sensitivity or risk analysis, and a business interpretation of results.
