# Decision Case

The learner must choose among at least two feasible but incompatible options.

- Establish one accountable protagonist, a deadline, and success criteria.
- Give each option meaningful benefits, costs, uncertainties, and affected stakeholders.
- Reveal evidence progressively; do not make one option obviously correct.
- End immediately before the decision, asking for choice, rationale, risks, and next actions.

Reject a case that only asks “what problems exist?” or resolves the choice in the narrative.
