# Ethical Dilemma Case

The learner must weigh duties or values that cannot all be fully satisfied.

- Give affected parties legitimate but conflicting claims.
- Separate legal compliance, professional duty, organizational interest, and personal consequence.
- Avoid villain framing and false binaries.
- Require a decision, ethical justification, mitigation for harmed parties, and communication plan.

Reject dilemmas solved by a simple rule quotation with no residual cost.
