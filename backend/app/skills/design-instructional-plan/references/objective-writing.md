# Objective Writing

Write each objective as: observable action + case object + reasoning method + assessable product or standard.

Select the reasoning framework:

- Pyramid principle: decision, comparison, recommendation, or structured argument tasks.
- Systems thinking: diagnosis, mechanism, feedback, recurring failure, or multi-party interaction tasks.
- 3W1H: implementation, communication, role simulation, or action planning tasks.

Create a three-step progression:

1. Establish and organize evidence.
2. Analyze or evaluate competing explanations/options.
3. Produce and defend an action or design.

Keep the teacher's topic and course concepts visible. Do not turn a case objective into a general course syllabus objective.
