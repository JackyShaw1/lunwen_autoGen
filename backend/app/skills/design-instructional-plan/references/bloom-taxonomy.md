# Cognitive Levels

| Level | Observable verbs | Suitable evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Understand | identify, classify, explain | accurate fact and role recognition |
| Apply | use, calculate, demonstrate | correct use of a concept in the case |
| Analyze | compare, distinguish, map, infer | causal chain or evidence grouping |
| Evaluate | judge, defend, prioritize | criteria-based choice with counterargument |
| Create | design, integrate, formulate | executable plan with measures and risks |

Avoid unobservable verbs such as know, understand, become familiar with, or master. Match the highest objective to the configured learner difficulty; do not make every objective “create”.
