# Discussion Ladder

Build questions in this order:

1. Evidence check: what happened, who knows what, and which facts are reliable?
2. Mechanism analysis: why did the situation emerge and what relationships sustain it?
3. Alternative evaluation: what options or explanations compete, using which criteria?
4. Decision challenge: what should the protagonist do now and what must be accepted or sacrificed?
5. Implementation and transfer: how should the decision be implemented, measured, or adapted elsewhere?

Every question must point to case evidence. Add a teaching intent that states the observable learner capability. Avoid yes/no questions and questions answered verbatim by the narrative.
